Who Can Fight

Eligibility & Rules

  • Eligibility Open to all undergraduate and postgraduate students from any branch and any year.
  • Squad Size 1 to 3 members per team. Solo entries are also accepted.
  • Cross-College Teams Allowed — teams can include members from different colleges.
  • Experience Level Beginners are welcome. No prior hackathon experience is required.
  • Registration Fee ₹200 per team.
  • Code of Conduct Respectful collaboration is expected. Plagiarized or pre-built projects will be disqualified.

Venue & Logistics

Host Campus: Mar Athanasios College For Advanced Studies Tiruvalla (MACFAST)

Format: Hybrid — build on campus or join remotely through the online track. Judging will be conducted live for both.

Provided: Wi-Fi, power outlets, workstation space, and mentor support throughout the event.

  • Bring your own laptop and charger
  • College or student ID is mandatory at check-in
  • All code must be written within the 6-hour hackathon window

FAQ
Do I need a team before I register?

Yes — squads of 2 to 4. If you don't have a full squad, list yourself as a solo applicant on the register page and we'll help you slot into one before the event.

Is there a registration fee?

Yes — a flat ₹200 per squad, whether you compete on-campus or remotely.

Can non-CS students join?

Absolutely. Vibe coding welcomes design, product, and no-code contributors — every squad just needs at least one member comfortable shipping code.

What does "vibe coding" mean here?

Building fast and iteratively with AI-assisted tools, rapid prototyping, and a focus on shipping a working demo over a polished pitch deck.
